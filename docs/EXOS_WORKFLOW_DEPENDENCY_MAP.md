## Workflow ownership findings

- All 16 workflows have a proposed owner, but six cross an unclear Sheets/Supabase authority boundary.
- Business rules are concentrated in `participant.py`, `programme_builder.py`, `live_event_console.py`, `google_sheets.py` and SQL migrations rather than dedicated domain services.
- The most urgent broken linkages are production identity migration/certification, backend-enforced submission authority, one canonical stage controller, and one report source.
