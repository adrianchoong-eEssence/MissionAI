# Genting V1 — inputs required before authorised content population

The local package is deliberately complete at the engine/configuration level, but these business decisions must be supplied by Adrian (or the designated Genting event owner) before it can be loaded into an event.

1. Event date, registration window, team-formation close time, Captain-selection time, hunt start/close time, and results announcement time.
2. Confirmed park access model: guest ticket/pass responsibility, authorised entry point, group check-in location, and whether the selected worlds are accessible during the exact event window.
3. Site-recce approval for every proposed opportunity-board mission: exact public-area SafeMeetingPoint, accessible alternative, maximum safe group footprint, operating-window availability, and a contingency if its location/world is unavailable.
4. Genting/operator approval for the final mission catalogue, including any restrictions on group photography, branding, costumes, commercial use, queue proximity, staff/guest inclusion, and Secret Mission release mechanics.
5. Final photo-evidence policy: participant consent wording, whether faces are required or optional, who may view evidence, retention/deletion period, storage jurisdiction, and escalation path for an objection or removal request.
6. Safety operating plan: named safety lead, facilitator-to-team ratio and placement, weather/rain/lightning rule, lost-participant process, first-aid/evacuation procedure, accessibility/mobility accommodations, and communications channel.
7. Final strategic mission catalogue: chosen ride(s), normal mission(s), bonus(es), secret(s), their zones/locations, exact wording/language variants, point values, evidence pathways, reference images, and approval/review rubric. The six current activities are placeholders only.
8. Facilitator review policy: reviewer identities/roles, target review turnaround, reject/resubmit wording, score-calibration method, and authority for recorded penalty deductions or tie resolution.
9. Competition policy: points for each mission class, any quality points, ride-abort/park-closure compensation treatment, prize/recognition, final rank/tie-break owner, whether standings remain hidden until Hunt Closed, and the exact audience/projector views to enable.
10. Operational scale confirmation: expected registrations beyond 66, treatment of no-shows/late arrivals, whether capacity remains exactly six teams of 11, and the separately required 66/250 real-concurrency certification outcome before release.
11. Authorised deployment decision: target environment, formal approval to install the existing 037 dependency and separately reviewed 038 OPEN_MISSION_BOARD source migration, and formal approval to create the live/staging event. None of those actions is included in this package.
